# Ohlone Publications News Website

A student-run news website created for **Ohlone College Publications**, a branch of Ohlone College Student Government. The platform provides a centralized place for students to read campus news, announcements, student stories, event coverage, and other publications relevant to the Ohlone community.

## About the Project

This website was built to support the **Publications branch of Ohlone College Student Government** by giving students an accessible and organized platform for campus news.

The goal of the project is to make important information easier to find while also giving student writers and contributors a place to share stories about campus life.

## Features

* Campus news and student articles
* Organized article and publication pages
* Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices
* Navigation for browsing different types of content
* Dedicated space for Student Government Publications
* Easy-to-read article layouts
* Centralized platform for Ohlone-related announcements and stories

## Purpose

Before this project, campus information could be spread across different platforms and communication channels. This website helps bring student-focused news into one place where students can quickly access updates and learn more about what is happening at Ohlone College.

The project also supports student journalism by providing a digital platform for publishing articles and highlighting student voices.
